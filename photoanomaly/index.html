Effective date: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JML Studio ("JML Studio", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Photo Anomaly mobile game (the "Application") on Android or iOS. Photo Anomaly is an ad-supported game that also offers an optional in-app purchase.

Information We Process

JML Studio does not require you to create an account and does not operate a user-profile database for the Application. However, third-party advertising, platform, and purchase services integrated into the Application may automatically process the following information:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and approximate location inferred from the IP address;
  • device identifiers, which may include the Android Advertising ID, Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), Identifier for Vendors (IDFV), and similar identifiers;
  • device and technical information, such as device model, operating system and version, language, screen characteristics, app version, network provider, carrier, and connection type;
  • advertising and usage information, such as ad impressions, ad clicks, interactions with ads, session information, and advertising performance;
  • diagnostic and performance information used to maintain, secure, and improve advertising delivery and Application performance.

The Application does not request or collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos or videos from your device, microphone recordings, or health data. The camera and photographs shown in the game are simulated gameplay features and do not access your device camera or photo library.

In-App Purchases

The Application offers an optional non-consumable in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Google Play on Android or Apple through the App Store on iOS, with Unity In-App Purchasing used for integration. These services may process a product identifier, transaction identifier, purchase status, and receipt or purchase token. JML Studio does not receive or store your full payment card or bank account details.
