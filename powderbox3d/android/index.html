Last Thylacine Powder Box 3D

Privacy Policy

Powder Box 3D (“the game”) for Android is made by Last Thylacine. This page lists what data the game uses and why. Questions: jamalfursov@gmail.com.

Playing the game

Saves, worlds and settings are stored on your device. The game does not ask for your location.

  • Ads: Appodeal and its ad partners show ads. They may receive your advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and ad interactions.
  • Analytics: GameAnalytics receives gameplay events, error reports, device model, operating system, app version and language. Advertising ID tracking is turned off in GameAnalytics.
  • Game settings: the game downloads a settings file from lastthylacine.com, or from GitHub Pages if that fails. These servers see your IP address.

Ad and analytics services may estimate your approximate location from your IP address. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Account

An account is optional. If you create one, our server stores:

  • your nickname and player ID;
  • your password, only as a one-way hash (it cannot be read back);
  • the date you signed up and your sign-ins on your devices.

We do not ask for your email, real name or phone number, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered.

The server logs the IP address of each request to stop password guessing and spam. These logs are kept for up to 14 days.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in the game (main menu → your nickname → “Delete account”) or on the account deletion page. The account is deleted at once. Its nickname stays reserved for 30 days, linked to no one, so nobody can pose as you. Server backups are deleted within 90 days.

Uninstalling the game removes its data from your device. To delete data held by Appodeal or GameAnalytics, contact them through their privacy pages below.

Services we use

  • Appodeal and its ad partners
  • GameAnalytics
  • Unity (game engine)
  • Cloudflare (our site and server run through it)
  • GitHub Pages (backup copy of the settings file)

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13.
