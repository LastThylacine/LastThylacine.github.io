Last Thylacine Powder Box 3D

Privacy Policy

Powder Box 3D (“the game”) for Android is made by Last Thylacine. This page lists what data the game uses and why. Questions: jamalfursov@gmail.com.

Playing the game

Saves, worlds and settings are stored on your device. The game shows ads and collects gameplay statistics through the services listed below. They receive device data and identifiers under their own privacy policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Account

An account is optional. If you create one, our server stores:

  • your nickname and player ID;
  • your password, stored only as a hash;
  • the date you signed up and your sign-ins on your devices.

We do not ask for your email, real name or phone number, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered.

Our servers log IP addresses to stop abuse. These logs are kept for up to 14 days.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in the game (main menu → your nickname → “Delete account”) or on the account deletion page. The account is deleted at once. Its nickname stays reserved for 30 days, linked to no one, so nobody can pose as you. Server backups are deleted within 90 days.
